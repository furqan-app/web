# 5.1 — Page face and reader

## Summary

Land the new language on the reader itself — the mushaf page face, the reading stage, the external page arrows, the reader chrome, and the recitation rail. This went last because every remaining correctness invariant in the project lives here.

The stage ships **flat**: after a visual-review round the radial lamp pool and peripheral vignette were removed entirely (eye-comfort — hotspot glare and tunnel-vision on long sessions). Depth is carried purely by physical book edges: stacked sheets, binding crease, rim, and a natural cast shadow. The page face is pure flat `--mushaf-paper`.

## Approach

Landed as commits kept separate for bisectability: **5.1a** the stage, **5.1b** the page face, **5.1c** the `768–1023px` band collapse, **5.1d** the recitation rail.

### (a) The stage — flat

Port the lab's composition to `/pages/[id]`: folio centring and the proportion cap, the quiet page arrows, the grounding cast. The **radial lighting is gone** — `.fq-reader-outer::before`/`::after` and the `.fq-safha-card` gradient pool were removed; the stage is a solid uniform `var(--viewer-background)` and the page is flat `--mushaf-paper`.

The height chain is the known trap: `height: 100%` inside a flex-grown box resolves to `auto` (ADR 0036). The folio cap took three attempts (both silent failures caught only by measuring the rendered box, not by reading the CSS): a `%`-height and a `max-height + margin-block: auto` on `.fq-spread-col` both measured content-height; the cap works one level **down**, on `.fq-spread` inside the stretched column, which by then has a definite used height.

### (b) The page face

0.2's derived mushaf token values landed in the four production theme blocks: paper raised on gold (`#f7f0de` → `#fdf9ee`) and dark (`#0C1117` → `#111820`) to clear chrome on the depth ladder; ornament/metadata moved to the identity bronze in every theme. **`--mushaf-text` is untouched** — 0.2 explicitly declined to change the ink.

Two theme-scoped `:root.theme-dark .fq-spread …` / `:root.theme-gold .fq-spread …` blocks that forced page metadata, the surah glyph, the inline surah name and the ornaments to `--mushaf-text` ("read quietly, not gold") were **deleted** — ornament is identity under the two-accent grammar, so their purpose was reversed, and at `(0,4,0)` they were also outranking the theme-agnostic rule. `.fq-ornament` (which 4.1 had turned into a 58×10 hairline box, invisible on the four screens 4.1 touched) was renamed `.fq-rule-mark`, its colour from a `--fq-mark` custom property so a mushaf caller can hand it `--mushaf-ornament`.

### (c) The `768–1023px` band collapse

Production split `768–1023` into its own inset single-page treatment; the lab recognises three device classes and overrides that band to full-bleed compact. The compact boundary moved 767 → 1023 across ten `globals.css` media queries and `MOBILE_QUERY`; the band's 16px desk margin was deleted, its leg of the desktop card-width clamp dropped, and page arrows are now CSS-gated out of compact (the JSX still carries `hidden md:flex`). `useIsMobile` widening is deliberate — its three consumers (`nav overlay`, `keep-screen-awake` toggle, its settings row) all read `isMobile || isTablet`.

**One deviation from the original plan:** reading size at `768–1023` moves 26px → **28px** (not "unchanged"). The band's size used to come from a desktop card-width clamp on the stored preset (default 26); it now comes from compact's own font-from-width formula, pinned to its 28px cap (ADR 0011). 28px is one of ADR 0054's three presets and the change is **upward**, so "reading size never shrinks to accommodate chrome" holds. A narrower consequence: a reader who chose 26 or 30 on wide desktop and narrows the window sees 28 (the desktop font control is `lg:`-gated and was already unreachable in this band).

### (d) The recitation rail

The rail is a **full-height 72px column** on the physical right, **icons only**, three zones (identity top / transport at the rail's true midpoint / tertiary utilities at the foot), separated by empty desk not a divider stack. `.fq-reader-spread-container` reserves `padding-right: 72px` — chrome reserves space, never overlaps the mushaf.

**One markup, two compositions.** The bar and the rail are the same JSX; the three zones are wrapped in `.fq-rail-zone` (`display: contents` everywhere except the desk band, where they become absolutely-positioned rail zones). Which zone a control belongs to is stated in markup, not inferred from source order, so the transport no longer drifts when the stop button appears/disappears. The rail reciter control is an identity medallion with one status dot from one state value — still the `ReciterCombobox` trigger (3.2 kept reciter switching). Play button uses `.fq-recitation-play` (translucent emerald face, emerald border, dynamic status ring).

A source-order specificity trap: `.fq-chrome-bar` is declared ~600 lines later at the same `(0,1,0)`, so it won the tie — the rail kept an opaque `background-color`, the horizontal bar's rim catch-light, and `--panel-cast`, while `background`/`box-shadow: none` sat in the file looking applied. Fixed by doubling the selector (`.fq-chrome-bar.fq-recitation-bar-rail`), not by moving the block.

### Header / footer margins

Header shifted toward the top card edge (`pt-1`) with `pb-3` before verse 1; footer toward the bottom (`pb-0`, outer `md:pb-1`) with `pt-2` top separation to clear line 15 / surah header frames. The 15 lines keep their exact proportions, line height, and font sizes. Mobile/tablet header & footer text inherit `text-muted-foreground` (the green `--mushaf-metadata` overrides for those bands were removed — desktop was already correct). Desktop reader outer min-height and rail top offset are `70px`.

## Decision Tree / Algorithm

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Height needs to travel below `.fq-reader-outer` | By `align-items: stretch`, never by `%` (ADR 0036). |
| A viewport-derived height is needed in the PWA path | Use the ICB-anchored fixed box, never `dvh`/`vh`/`svh`/`lvh` (ADR 0044). |
| Dark reader surface needs lift | Raised face + warm rim + real book edges. **Never radial lighting or a drop shadow** — removed for eye comfort; `--reader-chrome-shadow` is `none` in dark. |
| A depth rule would be theme-scoped | Change values, not rules (ADR 0032). |
| Any change would alter word placement, line count, or resolved font size | Stop. Correctness invariant, not an aesthetic. |
| Page arrows restyled | Must not enter, cover, dim, or clip Qur'an text. |
| A geometry check runs over the pager | Select **per card**, never per document — the pager mounts three spreads side by side. |

## Verified Test Cases

Every case in three themes, both locales, regular and tajweed:

| Case | Required outcome |
|---|---|
| Pages 1, 2–3, 187, 528, 604 at 1440 / 1200 / 1023 / 834 / 768 / 390 | **0 word overlaps, 0 escapes from the card box** (verified — 40 combinations); resolved font size constant per band: 26 (desk) / 35.48 (tablet, auto-fit) / 28 (compact) / 24.9 (390). |
| 768×1024 and 834×1112 | Full-bleed single page — the inset band is gone; reading size is 28px (see the deviation note). |
| Single vs double Safha view | Both correct; stored preference respected. |
| Mobile carousel swipe | No flicker on commit (ADR 0029); direction correct. |
| Tablet full-bleed | Edge-to-edge, no shadows, no desk margin. |
| Recitation rail at ≥1367×800 | 72×843 / 72×1043 anchored at the desk's right edge, full height nav→foot, 0 text pixels inside, no overlap with card or arrows. Below the band the full-width bar renders unchanged. |
| Surah frame | Correct ratio, `height: auto`, never stretched, one colour role. |
| Depth ladder `desk ≤ chrome < page` | Holds in all three themes (measured). |

**Not exercised here:** tajweed rendering, the vertical-pages route, the shared-grant reader, slow-network page turns, the installed-PWA fullscreen transition — each needs an edition switch, a device, or a throttled session. Visual baselines need the `workflow_dispatch` regeneration.

## Constraints

- Word placement, page dimension calculations, the font-size contract (ADR 0054), and the page font files are untouched.
- Never use `height: 100%` below `.fq-reader-outer`, or viewport units for the reader shell height (ADR 0036 / 0044).
- **No radial lighting, no drop shadow on a dark reader surface** — depth is physical book edges only. The page face is pure flat `--mushaf-paper`.
- Never stretch the surah frame art; `height: auto`, width drives size, one colour role.
- Depth rules are shared across themes; only values differ (ADR 0032). Verify by sampling rendered pixels in all three themes at every band — take the **max** lightness down a strip (not a point sample, which can land on the surah frame) and keep the strip away from the seam (the binding crease darkens the gutter).
- The reading size never shrinks to accommodate chrome.
- Land the stage, page face, band collapse, and rail as separate commits.
- Baselines regenerated only via the `workflow_dispatch` CI job — never local PNGs.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not reintroduce the radial lamp pool, the peripheral vignette, or any page-face gradient — removed for eye comfort ("do not reintroduce" was already on the earlier desk-pool removal, and this round removed the whole system).
- Do not use `height: 100%` below `.fq-reader-outer`, or viewport units for the reader shell height.
- Do not add a drop shadow to a dark reader surface.
- Do not change `--mushaf-text` unless 0.2 explicitly decided to.
- Do not restore the `:root.theme-dark/.theme-gold .fq-spread` blocks forcing metadata/glyph/ornament to `--mushaf-text` — ornament is identity; they reversed the grammar and outranked the fix.
- Do not restore the green `--mushaf-metadata` text overrides on the mobile/tablet safha header & footer — they inherit `text-muted-foreground`.
- Do not let page arrows overlap Qur'an text; do not let the rail overlap the spread or the arrows (`padding-right: 72px` reserves it).
- Do not treat a correct render at page 2 as evidence for 187 or 528; do not rely on regenerated snapshots to catch an overlap or rhythm regression.
- Do not run a geometry check over the pager per-document — select per card.

## Decisions Made

- The reader goes last — every other surface has by then proven the tokens, primitives, and chrome.
- The stage is **flat** — radial lighting and the page-face gradient pool were removed after visual review; depth is real book edges (stacked sheets, crease, rim, cast shadow). The page is pure `--mushaf-paper`.
- Stage / page face / band collapse / rail land as separate commits for bisectability. The lab's composition is ported, not re-derived (proven against these exact production components).
- 0.2's token values landed with `--mushaf-text` untouched; two theme-scoped `.fq-spread` override blocks deleted (they reversed the two-accent grammar and outranked the fix).
- The `768–1023` band is full-bleed compact; reading size there is 28px (up from 26 — an ADR 0054 preset; upward, so the shrink-for-chrome constraint holds). Flagged, not further resolved: a wide-desktop 26/30 preset is not honoured in this band.
- The recitation rail is a 72px full-height icons-only column with markup-declared zones; `padding-right: 72px` reserves its space; it stays the `ReciterCombobox` trigger.
- Mobile navbar shows the Settings icon directly (removed from `UserMenu`); `HomeContinueReadingCard` is a solid `bg-card` surface.
